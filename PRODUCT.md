# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is a person who strength trains independently and uses Dracox to plan and record gym sessions.

## Product Purpose

Dracox helps a person create flexible routines, record strength workouts, and review their training history and progress. Success means the next workout is easy to choose, session details are easy to capture while training, and records remain available afterward.

## Positioning

No distinctive market claim is confirmed. Describe the product by its actual routine, workout, and progress-tracking capabilities; do not invent a differentiator.

## Operating Context

- Users choose which routine day to train; the schedule is flexible rather than tied to fixed weekdays.
- The app is a responsive, mobile-first web app and installable PWA, intended to remain usable during gym sessions.
- Workout changes can be recorded offline and synchronized when connectivity returns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing capabilities include account and profile management; routine and exercise management; multi-day routine editing; live set, weight, and rep logging; rest timing; workout history; progress summaries and records; offline storage and synchronization; and an administrator error log.
- Preserve every existing capability and user record during this UI redesign. Present recommendations for removing or adding product capabilities separately for the user's decision.
- The current implementation uses Angular and Supabase.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the product name Dracox.

## Product Principles

- Make the next workout and in-session recording easy to find and use.
- Keep the complete workout flow reliable, including offline use and later synchronization.
- Preserve user-entered training data and every existing capability during interface changes.
- Communicate only product benefits supported by the current implementation.
